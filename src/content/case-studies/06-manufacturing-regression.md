#### Ownership and approach

Co-led the modeling project, using real-time and historical production data to identify the signals most strongly associated with layer thickness.

#### Important decisions and constraints

The analysis covered more than 50 furnace sensor signals in an industrial production process. Regression and statistical analysis were used to isolate useful predictors and connect the model findings to practical quality-control improvements.
