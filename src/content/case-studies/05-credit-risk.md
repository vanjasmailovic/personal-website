#### Ownership and approach

Built customer-facing credit-risk capabilities and supported pre-sales, onboarding, integration, and technical delivery for newly acquired customers, including a major customer acquisition.

#### Important decisions and constraints

Raw bank-transaction descriptions required reliable categorization before they could contribute to risk assessment. NLP transaction tagging supported downstream binary default models, while feature selection and SHAP were used for model development and explainability.
