#### Ownership and approach

Built the interactive map from public US machine-shop directory data, covering collection, cleaning, categorization, and the final visualization used for sales planning.

#### Important decisions and constraints

Public directory data needed to be transformed into a useful commercial view. Business prioritization logic categorized prospects by estimated potential, while geospatial presentation made the result practical for regional outreach planning.
