# Content review

This file records the content and presentation decisions made for the first production version. The CV and portfolio PDFs remain the factual source of truth.

## Copy adapted from the PDFs

- Hero positioning combines the CV summary’s product focus, 15+ years of experience, hands-on ownership, and technical leadership.
- The four service groups consolidate the seven portfolio service bullets without expanding their factual scope.
- Experience dates, role titles, acquisition history, leadership claims, education, research, and awards come from the CV.
- All seven case studies preserve the portfolio’s order and Problem / Built / Technology / Outcome structure.
- Case-study details about ownership, constraints, evaluation, safeguards, validation, and technical decisions are included only where supported by the CV.
- Outcome qualifiers such as “approximately,” “estimated,” “typical,” and “qualitative” are retained.

## Suggested rewrites used

- “Turn ambiguous AI opportunities into shipped product capabilities” is expressed in shorter, buyer-oriented language across the hero and services sections.
- The service list is grouped into AI assistants and analytics agents; document and knowledge automation; applied machine-learning products; and AI product discovery and delivery.
- The technology section is curated into four commercially relevant groups instead of reproducing the full CV inventory or portfolio word cloud.
- “Team of 2–4” and “team of 4” are reconciled as “a team of up to four,” following Vanja’s confirmation.

## Confirmed claims and permissions

- The anomaly-detection product may be described as the company’s “second most important product area.”
- The supplied `avatar_new.jpg` is approved for public website use.
- Company logos are approved for use as general experience evidence.
- Case studies must remain anonymized and must not be visually or verbally associated with specific employers or clients.
- Company names may appear in the separate professional-experience section.

## Conflicting facts

- Resolved: “second-largest product area” in the CV versus “second most important product area” in the portfolio. Confirmed wording: “second most important product area.”
- Resolved: “team of 2–4” in the CV versus “team of 4” in the portfolio. Confirmed wording: “a team of up to four.”

No unresolved factual conflict currently blocks publication.

## Omitted or condensed material

- Telephone number is omitted from the webpage. It remains inside the public CV PDF.
- Detailed responsibility and technology lists for every employer are condensed for page readability; the full CV remains linked.
- Individual publication titles and the full education timeline are condensed into an academic-foundation section.
- The short portfolio URL in the CV is not used; the site links directly to the clean public portfolio PDF path.
- Detailed tools described in the CV as basic exposure or familiarity are omitted from the curated capabilities section.

## Privacy and confidentiality

- Case-study narratives intentionally omit company and client names.
- Company logos appear only in the separate selected-experience section.
- The site does not include a contact form, telephone number, analytics, tracking, cookies, or a cookie banner.
- No account screenshots, DNS information, or hosting identifiers are included in the production site.

## Proposed design changes

- Dark, high-contrast visual system selected after review, with a restrained mint accent and no gradients.
- The supplied monochrome portrait is used as the hero visual.
- Approved company marks are shown in a consistent monochrome treatment.
- Case studies use outcome-led cards with native inline expansion instead of modals.
- Motion is limited to small hover transitions and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Information architecture

- Top-level navigation remains Home, Case Studies, and Contact.
- Home contains hero, quantified evidence, services, selected experience, leadership, capabilities, and academic foundation.
- All seven case studies remain on the main page with stable anchors and an index.
- Contact contains direct email, location, LinkedIn, CV, and portfolio links. No contact form is included.
